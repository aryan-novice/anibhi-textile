The Weaves of Banaras

Know your Banarasi

Banarasi is a family of weaving techniques, each with its own look and feel. A short guide to the names you will hear when choosing a saree.

KATAN

Pure silk threads twisted together for a smooth, lustrous base. The classic Banarasi silk.

KADHUA

Each motif is woven by hand on its own, with no loose threads at the back. Slow, prized work.

JANGLA

A dense, all-over pattern of vines and florals that covers the body of the saree.

TANCHOI

Several coloured weft threads woven in, giving a rich, brocade-like texture.

Every weave has a story. Ask us which techniques are in the current collection.
